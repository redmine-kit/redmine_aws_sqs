#### Jobs on Amazon SQS

With [Redmine Kit AWS SQS](../aws-sqs/jobs.md) and `REDMINE_SQS_QUEUE_URL` set, the queue adapter is the one of Amazon SQS, so this check passes. **Administration** › **AWS** › **Jobs** shows the queue and the jobs that failed.
